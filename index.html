Unity Grow · Product portfolio

From 40 services to 3 product pillars

Unity's growth business presented customers with 40 loosely connected services. The organization mirrored that fragmentation: separate teams, roadmaps, and competing definitions of the product.

I helped establish the core decision — Unity is the product; everything else is a feature — then used my design team's cross-product view to make the new portfolio concrete. The work forced the necessary tradeoffs: roadmaps were rewritten, an in-flight dashboard was scrapped, teams were reassigned, and the customer experience was rebuilt around three pillars.
