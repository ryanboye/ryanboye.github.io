// About me

Hi, I'm Ryan.

I’m a product and design leader based in Oakland, California. For nearly 20 years, I’ve worked at the edge of emerging technology, making complex products easier to understand and use.

I joined Cloudflare as one of its first 100 employees, helping make technical performance and security products accessible to a broader audience.

For the past seven years, I’ve been at Unity, most recently leading Product Design for Grow across advertising, monetization, and the core developer platform.

I built and led a team of 15 designers, and worked with the CEO and executive leadership to use Design and storytelling to reshape the business through a major turnaround.

More recently, I moved into a hands-on role leading product for Unity’s next generation of agentic game development—from strategy and research through prototyping and development.

I’m passionate about building great teams, bringing Design into product and business strategy, and making powerful technology accessible to more people.
