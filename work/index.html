Areas my team covered

From acquisition and monetization products to the shared systems and web experiences around them.

Monetization

Tools for developers to earn revenue by showing ads. We redesigned the mediation experience, making a complex ML-driven system accessible to everyone from solo indie creators to large studios with dedicated monetization teams.

Years of interviews exposed a durable job to be done: developers wanted more ways to make money. We championed a roadmap spanning IAP, offerwall, new ad formats, and a more connected monetization system.

User Acquisition

Campaign tools for advertisers acquiring users for their apps. We redesigned campaign creation to balance advertiser control with algorithmic optimization, and rebuilt reporting so advertisers could connect spend to outcomes like retention and ROAS.

We made the relationship between customer actions, shared signals, targeting, and campaign performance visible. Clear benefits and informed choices increased participation while improving outcomes for customers and the platform.

Offerwall

Users engage with offers in exchange for in-app rewards. We redesigned both sides: the consumer experience inside games and the publisher dashboard. A three-sided design challenge — native to the app, valuable to the user, performant for the advertiser.

Analytics & Reporting

The analytics layer that ties every product together. We built unified reporting for publishers and advertisers — each with different mental models for what "performance" means — surfacing actionable insights rather than raw data.

Selected analytics launches Funnels & SQL Data Explorer Mar 2022 ↗ One home for UA & monetization performance May 2024 ↗

Ad Format Innovation

New ad experiences beyond standard interstitials and banners — playable ads, interactive end cards, and formats that blur the line between content and promotion. More engaging for users, more effective for advertisers, more lucrative for publishers.

Our teams used A/B testing to continually improve formats, interactions, and incentives. Many of those design-led experiments produced meaningful conversion gains through repeated iteration rather than occasional large redesigns.

Public product story The power of play: unlocking growth with playables Oct 2025 ↗

Unified platform experience

Six products built by different teams with different stacks and design conventions. We built a shared design system and drove adoption by treating it as a product, not a mandate — making it genuinely faster to use than to build from scratch.

Core Platform Experience

Across Unity's dashboard ecosystem, customers encountered products built by different teams, stacks, and conventions. We brought development, user acquisition, and monetization into a clearer shared experience—helping customers understand the platform as one connected journey.

Unity.com

I led design for the Unity web platform—the templates, navigation, and information hierarchy that structure how millions of developers discover and engage with Unity's products.

Across navigation, signup, product routing, and onboarding, we repeatedly removed friction and clarified the next action. Those gains compounded into more—and better-qualified—customers reaching Unity's core products.
