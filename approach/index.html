Leadership

The discipline

The role of Design

Design earns its influence by connecting the parts of the business that customers experience as one product.

Design connects the decisions no single team owns.

Product and engineering are usually organized vertically around teams, systems, and roadmaps. Design is the horizontal counterpart: it sees how those decisions accumulate across the customer journey, brings the voice of the customer into the room, and holds the whole experience to a coherent standard of craft and quality.

01

See the whole journey.

Designers work across product silos by nature. That puts us in a position to catch the patterns, gaps, and contradictions that no single vertical team is positioned to see.

02

Turn customer understanding into direction.

The work is not only to document customer needs. It is to turn that understanding into a practical point of view about what should be built, how the pieces should fit together, and what would actually be better.

03

Show what unified looks like.

Showing is our craft. At Unity, my team's product visions made an abstract portfolio strategy tangible enough to rally teams, rewrite roadmaps, and replace fragmented work with one connected experience.
