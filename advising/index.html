Advising.

I help founders clarify the product, the customer, and the story.

I work with a small number of founders and startup teams—usually a regular call where we work through whatever's hardest right now. Product strategy, positioning, hiring your first designer, figuring out why customers aren't converting. The kind of conversation that saves you months of going in the wrong direction.

How it works

A conversation, not a deliverable

I don't produce decks or design artifacts. I get on a call, learn your situation, and help you think through it. Most founders tell me a single conversation reframed how they were thinking about the problem.

Regular cadence or as-needed

Some clients book a standing monthly or bi-weekly call. Others reach out when they hit a wall. Both work. We'll figure out the right rhythm for your stage.

$2,000 flat fee

One monthly rate, no hourly tracking. Includes regular calls and async access between sessions. Simple and predictable for both of us.

What I help with

Product strategy

You have a product but it's not clicking. We figure out why — is it the positioning, the UX, the market, or the problem you chose to solve?

Finding product-market fit

You're pre-PMF and burning runway. I help you narrow the aperture — who's your real customer, what's their real problem, what's the simplest thing you can build to prove it?

Pitch & positioning

You can't explain what you do in one sentence. I help you find the story — for investors, for customers, for your own team.

Hiring & building design

You're hiring your first designer or building a small team. I help you figure out what kind of designer you actually need and how to set them up to succeed.

Selected advising

Helping teams find the sharper version of the idea.

Every company arrives with a different knot to untangle. I help founders see the customer more clearly, make the difficult product choices, and tell a story that gives the team—and its investors—a reason to believe.
